# Equipment and items

*World → Game system*

## Fields

1. **Illustration** — image of the item
2. **Value** — price in GP, SP, CP (factor 10 between each)
3. **Weight** — item weight (free numeric string, in the world's unit)
4. **Size** — item size per the D&D 5.5 SRD
5. **Rarity** — one value among: Common, Uncommon, Rare, Very rare, Epic, Legendary, Unique
6. **Level** — equipment level or level required to use it
7. **Public description** — visual, olfactory… description of the item, associated rules and effects
8. **Private description** — secrets and details of the item
