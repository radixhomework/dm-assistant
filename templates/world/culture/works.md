# Works

*World → Culture*

## Fields

1. **Illustration** — image of the work
2. **Origin** — place of origin of the work (single reference to a Place)
3. **Public description** — description of the work for the players
4. **Private description** — secrets and details of the work
5. **Events** — events associated with the work
6. **Characters** — characters and entities associated with the work
7. **Organizations** — organizations associated with the work
