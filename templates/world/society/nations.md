# Nations

*World → Society*

## Fields

1. **Illustration** — image of the nation
2. **Population** — number of inhabitants
3. **Political system** — one value among: Anarchy, Aristocracy, Autocracy, Confederation, Diarchy, Empire, Matriarchy, Militocracy, Monarchy, Oligarchy, Patriarchy, Republic, Tribal system, Theocracy, Timocracy, Triarchy
4. **Public description** — summary describing the nation to players
5. **Private description** — secrets and details of the nation
6. **Capital** — single reference to a Place
7. **Geography** — places associated with the nation
8. **Beliefs** — beliefs, myths and customs associated with the nation
9. **Works** — works associated with the nation
10. **Knowledge** — knowledge and technologies associated with the nation
11. **Languages** — languages spoken in the nation
12. **Characters** — characters and entities associated with the nation
13. **Organizations** — organizations associated with the nation
14. **Events** — events associated with the nation
