# Organizations

*World → Society*

## Fields

1. **Illustration** — image of the organization
2. **Member count** — a single or approximate figure
3. **Public description** — summary describing the organization to players
4. **Private description** — secrets and details of the organization
5. **Headquarters** — single reference to a Place
6. **Leaders** — characters and entities leading the organization
7. **Characters** — characters and entities associated with the organization
8. **Organizations** — other associated organizations (self-reference)
9. **Events** — events associated with the organization
